# Roadmap

Incremental and iterative development, organised in two-week sprints.

| Iteration | Dates | Goal | Deliverable |
|---|---|---|---|
| I0 | 16 Nov – 13 Dec 2026 | Planning and analysis | Requirements, backlog, configured tools |
| I1 | 14 Dec 2026 – 17 Jan 2027 | Design and technical base | ER diagram, OpenAPI, prototypes, base app and API |
| I2 | 18 Jan – 14 Feb 2027 | Inventory | Version 0.1 |
| I3 | 15 Feb – 14 Mar 2027 | Alerts and finances | Version 0.2 |
| I4 | 15 Mar – 11 Apr 2027 | Sales with scanner | Version 0.3 |
| I5 | 12 Apr – 16 May 2027 | Verifactu invoicing | Version 0.4 and test report |
| I6 | 17 May – 6 Jun 2027 | Export, website and pilot | Version 1.0 and pilot report |
| I7 | 7 Jun – 25 Jun 2027 | Closure | Final report, user manual, presentation |
